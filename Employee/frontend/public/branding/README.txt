Place your branding images in this folder.

Required filenames (used by the app):
- logo.png                -> shown on Welcome page (recommended 512x512 or larger, square)
- favicon.png             -> browser tab icon (48x48 or 64x64, square)
- apple-touch-icon.png    -> mobile home screen icon (180x180, square)

After adding the files, hard refresh the app (Ctrl+Shift+R).
